# Badminton scoring rules, for singles and doubles

In badminton, every rally scores a point, and the score also tells you who serves and from which half of the court. The system changed in 2026: games are moving from 21 points to 15. Here are the scoring rules set by the Badminton World Federation (BWF), with worked examples.

## The new format: games to 15

At its Annual General Meeting on 25 April 2026, the BWF adopted a new scoring system known as 3x15. It enters the Laws of Badminton on 4 January 2027. Until then, the Laws in force still use games to 21.

Some national federations switched earlier. In France, the French Badminton Federation (FFBaD) has applied 3x15 since 1 September 2026 to every competition it organises or authorises, tournaments included.

The 21-point format does not disappear: the BWF keeps it as an alternative scoring system, to be agreed in advance, and the FFBaD keeps it for its Promobad events.

## Match format

A match is played as the **best of three games**: the first player or pair to win two games wins the match. There are therefore two or three games.

Each game is played to **15 points**. The side winning a game serves first in the next game.

## A point on every rally

Every rally scores a point for whoever wins it, **whether they served or not**: this is rally-point scoring. A rally is won when the shuttle lands inside the opponent's court, or when the opponent commits a fault: shuttle out, into the net, hit twice in a row, and so on.

A shuttle landing **on the line is in**.

## Winning a game: 15 points, a two-point lead, a cap at 21

1. The first side to 15 points wins the game, provided it leads by **two points**. At 15-13, the game is over.
2. At **14-all**, play continues until one side leads by two points: 16-14, 17-15, and so on.
3. At **20-all**, the next point decides: the side scoring the 21st point wins the game, 21-20.

A game can therefore never go beyond 21 points.

## Who serves, and from which side

The side that wins the rally **serves the next point**. The service court depends on the server's own score:

- **even** score (0, 2, 4…): serve from the **right** service court;
- **odd** score (1, 3, 5…): serve from the **left** service court.

The serve always goes diagonally, into the opposite service court.

A singles example. Lea serves at 0-0 from the right. She wins the rally: 1-0, she now serves from the left. Tom wins the next rally: 1-1, so he serves, from his left because his score is odd. Tom wins again: 2-1 to Tom, and he serves from the right.

## Doubles

In doubles, the odd or even rule applies to the serving side's score.

- When the serving side wins the rally, **the same player serves again** and the two partners swap service courts.
- When the receiving side wins the rally, it takes over the serve **without its players changing places**. The server is the player standing in the service court matching the side's new score: right if even, left if odd.

So only a serving side that wins the rally changes position.

## Changing ends and taking a break

Players change ends:

- at the end of the first game;
- at the end of the second game, if there is a third;
- in the third game, when a side first reaches 8 points.

The Laws also allow an interval of up to 60 seconds in each game when the leading score reaches 8 points, and of up to 2 minutes between games.

## The previous 21-point format

Under the previous format, games went to 21 points: a two-point lead was needed from 20-all, with a cap at 30, the 30th point winning the game at 29-all. In the third game, players changed ends at 11 points, and the 60-second interval also came at 11 points.

In a club or among friends, nothing stops you agreeing on another format, as long as everyone agrees before you start.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many points do you need to win a game of badminton?

15 points with a two-point lead under the 3x15 system: at 14-all, play goes on until one side leads by two, and at 20-all the 21st point wins. Under the previous format, games went to 21.

### When did badminton switch to games of 15 points?

The BWF Laws make 3x15 the standard from 4 January 2027. Some federations switched earlier: France on 1 September 2026, for all competitions run or authorised by the FFBaD.

### Who serves after a point in badminton?

The side that just won the rally. It serves from the right if its score is even, and from the left if it is odd.

### When do players change ends in badminton?

At the end of each game (the second only if there is a third), and in the third game when a side reaches 8 points. Under the previous 21-point format, this change came at 11 points.

### Is a shuttle that lands on the line in?

Yes. A line is part of the area it marks out, so a shuttle touching it is in.
